Business details

Start with the account and organization details. These are sent to the platform admin for review before your business tools are unlocked.

Are there any services that are available only during a specific time, or are all services available throughout your opening hours?

Choose this based on how your services actually run. If every service follows your normal business hours, choose the first option. If some services have separate schedules, choose the second option and add examples so the admin understands your setup.

Do you need to collect customer information before issuing a token?

Use this if your staff needs customer details before serving them. For example, a clinic may need a name and phone number, while a quick repair counter may only need a token number.

Which details should customers enter?

Which selected fields should be required?

Should staff be allowed to recall a token from the counter screen?

This is useful in noisy or busy locations. For example, a bank counter can call A005 again if the customer missed the first announcement.

Do you want to group services by category?

Categories help customers choose the right service faster. A hospital might use Consultation, Pharmacy, Laboratory, and Payments. A salon might use Hair, Nails, Skin Care, and Bridal Services.

Do you want to allow customers to access queue updates using mobile or QR access?

Choose yes if customers should be able to scan a QR code or use their phone to follow their turn from outside the waiting area.

Do you want to allow customers to make online bookings through the app?

Bookings are useful when customers need a planned date or time, such as consultations, salon appointments, repairs, or scheduled office visits. You can choose maybe later if you want to start with queues first.

How should online booking time slots work?

Pick a general booking slot length and how many customers can book the same slot. For example, a doctor may accept 1 booking every 15 minutes, while a service center may accept 5 bookings every 60 minutes.

Ready to submit

Your business details and preferences will be sent to the platform admin for review. These answers help the admin understand how your organization wants to use Waitless, without forcing you to configure services, counters, staff, bookings, or kiosks right now.
