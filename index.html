Smart queue management without the maze

Start with who you are. Waitless handles the right workflow.

Business owners manage queues, customers get or track tokens, staff serve counters, and kiosks issue self-service tokens.

Business Manage my organization Set up services, counters, kiosks, bookings, reports, and settings. Customer Join, book, or track Find an organization, get a token, book an appointment, or track status. Staff Serve my counter Call, recall, transfer, complete, or skip tokens from one focused screen. Kiosk Open kiosk mode Launch the customer-facing token screen for a registered kiosk device.

Simple operating model

Every workspace has one job.

1

Business sets up

Create services, counters, assignments, kiosk devices, open hours, and booking rules.

2

Customers get tokens

Customers use the web portal or kiosk to join a queue, book ahead, and track progress.

3

Staff serve smoothly

Counter staff see only their live serving tools, while dashboards and displays update in realtime.
